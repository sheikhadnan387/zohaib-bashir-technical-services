# SEO keyword research — Dubai

**Source:** Google Ads keyword data for the UAE (via Porter / DataForSEO), pulled 14 Oct 2026. Volumes are the monthly average over the last 12 months, rounded by Google. Where Google groups close variants (e.g. "carpet shampoo dubai" = "carpet cleaning dubai") they share one number. **Blank = too small for Google to report.** CPC is what advertisers pay per click, a proxy for how valuable a lead is.

Competition: **L**ow / **M**edium / **H**igh (paid competition, a decent proxy for how crowded the organic results are).

## What not to chase

- **Neighbourhood pages** ("sofa cleaning dubai marina" = 30, "ac repair business bay" = 10). Volumes are tiny, and most area combinations show nothing. Use the area names as on-page text and in the Google Business Profile service area, not as separate pages. Exception: "ac repair jvc" (210).
- **Arabic.** Highest is صيانة مكيفات دبي at 170/mo (growing); cleaning terms are 20–30. Not worth a translated site yet; revisit once the English site ranks.
- **"Near me" terms** can't be won with page text. They're won with a **Google Business Profile** and reviews.

## Seasonality (plan content dates around this)
- **Cleaning:** big spike **January–February** ("carpet cleaning dubai" hit 8,100 and "deep cleaning dubai" 12,100 in Jan 2026) — likely pre-Ramadan/holiday deep-cleaning. Have pages live and reviewed by **December**.
- **AC:** peaks **May–September** (some terms up to 8,100 in June). Be ranking by **April**.
- **Painting:** steady all year, bumps in Sep–Nov.

## Recommended changes to the site

1. **Rename the services to match how people search**, with 301 redirects from the old URLs (safe now — the site isn't indexed yet):
   | Now | Becomes | New URL |
   |---|---|---|
   | Shampoo Cleaning | Sofa & Carpet Cleaning | `/services/sofa-carpet-cleaning` |
   | Painting Work | Painting Services | `/services/painting-services` |
   | AC Services | AC Repair & Maintenance | `/services/ac-repair-maintenance` |
   | Flooring & Parquet Work | Parquet & Wooden Flooring | `/services/parquet-wooden-flooring` |
2. **Rewrite titles, meta descriptions, H1s and intros** around each page's primary keyword (above), keeping the brand and the word "shampoo" in the body copy.
3. **Homepage title:** "Technical Services Dubai | AC Repair, Painting, Cleaning & Flooring".
4. **Add sub-service pages** for terms with real volume that the site already describes: AC duct cleaning (1,300), AC cleaning (1,000), curtain cleaning (590), mattress cleaning (480).
5. **Update `keywords`, JSON-LD service names, sitemap, internal links and the footer/menu** to the new names.
6. **Off-site (matters more than any page for "near me"):** claim and complete the Google Business Profile — primary category, all four services, service areas, photos, hours; collect 10+ real reviews; keep name/address/phone identical everywhere (directories, social).

## Honest expectations
- No one can guarantee #1 in Dubai. These are competitive, ServiceMarket / Justlife / Urban Company-type aggregators own much of page one for the cleaning and AC terms.
- Fastest realistic wins: **flooring, parquet, villa painting, painting services** (low–medium competition), plus **"technical services dubai"**.
- Slower, needs reviews and a Business Profile: **AC and cleaning**.
- A brand-new domain usually takes 3–6 months to show movement.

## Still needs the client (these move rankings most)

### Google Business Profile checklist
"Near me" searches (~10,000/mo across AC, carpet, sofa, painter) are won here, not on the website.
1. Claim the profile at business.google.com and complete verification.
2. **Primary category:** pick the one that matches the biggest service (e.g. "Air conditioning repair service"); add the others as secondary categories.
3. Add all four services, each with a short description using the keywords above.
4. Set the **service area** (the 12 areas in `company.ts`, once confirmed) — hide the street address if customers don't visit.
5. Upload real photos of work, the team and the van; add the logo.
6. Add the website, phone and WhatsApp; set opening hours.
7. Ask every finished customer for a review (send the Google review link by WhatsApp); aim for 10+ in the first two months and reply to each.
8. Keep **name, address, phone identical** to the website everywhere (directories, Facebook, Instagram).
